Shape your career with us

A career
without borders

We hire lawyers and support staff who want their work to have reach. You will work on matters that cross jurisdictions from the very first week, with the training and the responsibility to match.

Explore opportunities

Our firm's history

  1. 1901The firm is founded in New York by a group of lawyers working on cross-border finance.
  2. 1958London and Paris open, establishing the European office network.
  3. 1996Asia-Pacific offices open in Tokyo, Hong Kong and Sydney.
  4. 2011The firm moves to a single global partnership structure across all offices.
  5. 2021A new identity is launched, uniting 40 years of practice areas into one firm.
  6. 202645 offices, 31 countries and more than 2,000 lawyers.
What you will do

Challenging work across borders

A single matter will often involve several offices, several legal systems and a client whose business does not recognise borders either.

01

Work on the transaction

You will draft, negotiate and close — not just observe. New joiners run their own workstreams in the first year.

02

Work across offices

Cross-office matters are the norm. Your colleagues in other cities are not colleagues you email — they are colleagues you work with.

03

Work with a partner

Every team has a partner responsible for your development, with a written plan reviewed twice a year.

Global experience and exposure

One firm,
everywhere

Trainees are offered a global rotation, and every lawyer has access to the firm's full client and knowledge base regardless of where they sit. Training is delivered locally, but the syllabus is the same in all 45 offices.

Our graduate programme runs a two-year rotation across practice groups, with a seat-based offer at the end of it. Support staff have their own career ladder and qualification support.

Our values

What we hold to

Pioneering

We look for the question behind the question. New technology, new regulation and new structures are assumed to be solved from the start, not retrofitted later.

United

One firm means one standard. Whatever office you join, the quality bar, the training and the way we treat clients are the same.

Human

Our clients are people and so are our colleagues. We build long careers by being straightforward with each other and by making room for lives outside the firm.

The day to day

Working here

Hybrid, with a reason

Two anchor days in the office, the rest as the matter demands. Client work wins over fixed schedules.

Learning

Structured training, a qualified tutor and a partner who has time for you. Not a reading list.
