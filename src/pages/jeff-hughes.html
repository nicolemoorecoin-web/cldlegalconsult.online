Jeff Hughes

Criminal defence — investigations, trials and appeals for people under arrest.

Jeff Hughes defends people accused of serious criminal offences. He has spent eighteen years in criminal practice, and he now acts for individuals and families facing investigation, arrest, extradition and trial in Washington and across the United States.

His work usually begins before there is a charge. Whether the first contact is a question at an airport, a knock on the door, or a telephone call from a police station, the first hours decide more than the trial does. Jeff builds the file from that first hour: what was searched, what was taken, who was present and what was said.

He is a trial lawyer who tries cases rather than negotiating them away, and he prepares every matter as though it will be heard by a jury — because a case prepared that way is a case that settles well. Before establishing CLD Legal Consultant in Seattle, Jeff was a partner in the criminal defence and investigations practice of two international firms.

Airport drug case — criminal defence representation

Jeff is currently representing a client in an airport-related drug case. The specific allegations, the jurisdiction and court, and the procedural status have not yet been established. The matter turns on several legal issues, which depend on the facts and the charges involved:

  • Search and seizure — the circumstances in which authorities searched the client, their baggage or other property, and whether the search complied with applicable law.
  • Evidence assessment — the collection, documentation, testing and handling of any substances or other physical evidence.
  • Applicable criminal law — the charged offence, the elements it requires, and the evidence available to establish them.
  • Procedural considerations — how the investigation and prosecution are being run under the rules applicable to the court handling the case.
  • Defence preparation — the potential legal challenges, preparation for court proceedings, and the appropriate defence strategies.

The defence approach will depend on the specific allegations, the evidence, the governing law and the stage of the proceedings.
