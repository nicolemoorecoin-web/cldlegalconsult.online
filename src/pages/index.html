About us

The firm
we are

CLD Legal Consultant is built on a simple idea: the most valuable thing a client can have on a complicated matter is one person who has read everything. Jeff Hughes leads every instruction personally, backed by a network of 2,000+ lawyers across 45 offices in 31 countries when a matter needs more hands.

Criminal defence is where the practice began, and it is what our principal is known for. But the people who call a firm rarely have only one problem — which is why fourteen other practice areas sit around it, and why one firm covers the lot.

That combination — senior attention on every file, and no limit to the bench behind it — is what lets us take on work that larger firms hand to a team you never meet.
