Principal Consultant

Jeff Hughes

Mergers & acquisitions, private capital, and the negotiations that decide what a company becomes.

Jeff Hughes advises founders, investors and boards on the transactions that decide what a company becomes. Over eighteen years he has led more than ninety cross-border mergers and acquisitions, with a consistent focus on the point where a deal stops being a set of documents and starts being an integration problem.

He built his practice around a simple idea: a client does not want a lawyer, they want fewer surprises. That means plain-language advice, an honest view of what is likely to go wrong, and a team small enough to answer the phone.

Before establishing CLD Legal Consultant in Seattle, Jeff was a partner in the corporate and private capital groups of two international firms, and before that a secondee in London and Singapore.
