Our firm

Galvanised
by complexity

CLD Legal Consultant is built on a simple idea: the most valuable thing a client can have on a complicated matter is one person who has read everything.

Our principal is a criminal defence lawyer, and it is the work he is known for — the matters where the evidence decides the outcome and the first hours matter most. But the people who call a firm rarely have only one problem, so fourteen other practice areas sit around it, and one firm covers the lot.

That is what sets us apart: senior attention on every file, a bench of 2,000+ lawyers behind it, and a genuinely collaborative way of working across 45 offices.

Responsible business

How we behave

We publish our responsible business review each year, covering our pro bono and legal aid work, our client commitments and our approach to the communities where we operate.

A fixed share of the firm's time goes to matters where the client cannot pay — the people who need a defence lawyer most are the people least able to afford one. We measure ourselves against the same commitments we ask clients to make: measurable targets, published progress, and an honest account of what has not worked.
